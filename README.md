I'm an engineering manager in Bengaluru with about 15 years of experience building payment and compliance systems. Right now I lead engineering for sanctions screening and core payments at TerraPay, a cross-border payments company.

Most of my work sits where money movement meets regulation: checking transactions against sanctions lists, connecting with partner banks and wallets, and keeping all of it fast and auditable. Lately I've been spending a lot of time on how to use LLMs safely in that kind of environment.

## What I'm working on

- Leading 14 engineers across three teams: screening, wallet integrations and platform.
- **TerraPayLens**, our sanctions screening platform. It runs as 27 microservices on AWS and checks transactions against 38+ global watchlists, around the clock.
- Using GPT-4 and Claude with RAG to improve name and address matching, inside an environment audited for SOC 2, ISO 27001 and PCI DSS.

## Things I'm proud of

- Integrated 80+ partner banks and wallets, including SWIFT and SEPA rails.
- Shipped a mobile money platform for an African telecom operator that now serves 4M+ subscribers at 99.9% uptime.
- Brought new-engineer onboarding down from three weeks to under one, mostly by writing things down properly.
